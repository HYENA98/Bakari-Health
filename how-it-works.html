How It Works

Your wellness journey in four simple steps.

At Bakari-Health, we make luxury IV therapy simple, safe and convenient. Whether you're at home, in the office or on the go, we bring wellness to you.

1

Choose Your Drip

Browse our menu of premium IV drips tailored to your wellness goals.

Whether you need an energy boost, immune support, beauty enhancement or recovery after a big event — we have a drip designed for you.

2

Book Your Session

Schedule your appointment at a time and place that suits you.

Choose your preferred date and time, and let us know your location. We'll handle the rest — no waiting rooms, no hassle.

3

Consultation & Assessment

A Registered Nurse will assess your health history and wellness goals.

We take your safety seriously. Our nurse will review your medical history, discuss any concerns and recommend the best drip for your needs.

4

Wellness Delivered

Relax and enjoy your IV therapy — from the comfort of your space.

Your drip is administered by a qualified nurse using sterile, premium-quality fluids. Simply sit back, relax and let the revitalisation begin.
